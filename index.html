Things to explore

This page has a few hidden tricks. Here is everything you can poke at.

Crew

Scuba crew

At the surface a welder patches a floating card, a mechanic tightens the depth gear and a swimmer keeps an eye on the fish. Two idle cleaners hover at the edges, then swim across to scrape the reef growth off the page. Scroll deep and they kick upward and vanish; in night mode their torches switch on.

Reef

Growing interface

Lichen, barnacles and seagrass colonise the headline, buttons, stats and cards. Give the cleaners a moment and they will scrape a patch clean. It slowly grows back.

Tide

Ripple at dusk

Switch between day and night and the new light spreads from the toggle like a stone dropped in water, ring after ring.

Click

Feed the fish

Click or tap empty water and food sinks down. Nearby fish swim over and eat it.

/

Sonar search

Press / or start typing in search. A sonar ring pings out, matching cards glow and the rest fade.

S

Submarine mode

Press S (or tap the sub icon in the footer). The page goes dark and a little sub lights the way with a flashlight.

Scroll

Depth meter

The gauge on the right edge counts your dive from the surface to the Mariana Trench and names each ocean zone as you pass through it.

Click

Show more

The shelf shows the first few projects with a progress bar. Hit Show more to float the rest into view, or Show fewer to tuck them away again.

Listen

Whale songs

Scroll into the deep and whale calls drift over the music now and then, more often the deeper you go. They also play in Marine Watch.

Click

Water drips

Every click or tap plays a little water drip. Switch it off in the music panel if you prefer quiet.

Look

Barnacles

Project cards carry a little cluster of barnacles in the corner, as if they have been down here a while.

W

Marine Watch

Press W or the eye button. The page fades away so you can just watch the ocean. Music keeps playing and you can still scroll.

Frame

Camera frame

In Marine Watch the page clears to a clean cinema frame and the crew turns filmmaker: a diver drops food for the school while you descend. No panels, just water.

Shake

Bubble storm

Shake your phone and the whole screen fills with rising bubbles and a rushing underwater sound that fades with them.

Rod

Go fishing

In Marine Watch, click the water to cast a line. When a fish bites, click again to reel it in.

Feed

Feed the deep

Drop your catch with Feed catch, then scroll down. Sharks, whales and other deep creatures swim over to eat it.

Night

Dive into the dark

The deeper you scroll in Marine Watch, the darker the water gets, until only the glowing creatures light it up.

Hover

Spooked fish

Move your cursor through the water and the fish scatter. Click a card and they dart away.

Scroll

Meet the deep

Scroll down and the sea gets deeper. Glowing turtles, sharks, a whale, a manta ray and an anglerfish drift by, lit up like the deep sea in Avatar.

Hover

Drifting interface

Headings, buttons and cards bob like they're underwater and lean away from your pointer.

Toggle

Day and night

Flip the sun/moon switch in the top bar to change the whole ocean from deep sea to shallows.

Music

Background music

The player in the top bar plays "Tu Mil Jaaye King". Open it to seek, mute or change the volume.

Click

Screenshot viewer

Click any card's image to open it large, then use the arrow keys to flip between projects.

Filter

Grid or list

Switch the shelf layout, sort it, or filter by type and tag chips.
